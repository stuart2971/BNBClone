About this space

Welcome to Casa Daniela, a spacious ground-floor condo half a block from the beach and a short stroll from the Puerto Morelos town square. One of the larger units in the Abbey del Sol collection, it was our featured Vacation Rental of the Month and suits families, couples and remote workers equally well.

The king bedroom has a new plush bed with 500-thread-count sheets, and a second bedroom sleeps two more. A queen sleeper sofa in the living area brings the total to six. Air conditioning runs throughout, and the washer and dryer are right in the unit.

Head up to the rooftop patio for panoramic views over the Caribbean Sea and the mangrove forest. Take a dip in the infinity pool, stretch out on a lounger, or eat under the palapa as the sun goes down. Free bikes are included, so exploring town is easy.

Our office is three blocks away and open 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. daily. The team is bilingual and can arrange airport transfers, reef snorkelling, cenote trips and tours to Tulum, Ek Balam or Chichén Itzá. Guests staying eight nights or more get a complimentary mid-stay clean.
